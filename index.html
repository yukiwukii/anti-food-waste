The proposal

Why we built this, and what we expect to happen.

Problem

Food is left over at the end of the day

Food-court vendors cook more than they sell, and the surplus is thrown away at closing.

Proposed solution

A food bin with a scale and cameras

  • The bin measures food waste by weight and identifies the dish with a camera.
  • The data tells vendors their average daily consumption, so they can cook less surplus.
  • The food waste is then transferred to a compost bin.
1 · Enforcement and public acceptance
  • The solution targets vendors, so enforcement and public acceptance are not an issue.
  • Vendors should be open to it, because it saves food preparation costs.
  • Bins in common eating areas such as North Spine and South Spine are convenient for consumers to use.
2 · Unintended consequences
  • Vendors have additional work if they use the bin. We think the long-term savings are worth it.
  • Data is incomplete because some customers take their food away. Some data is still better than no data.
3 · Root cause

Vendors find it hard to estimate daily food consumption because they do not have enough data. A food-waste bin that collects this data addresses the cause directly.
