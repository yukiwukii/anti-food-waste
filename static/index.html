What each vendor should cook and serve

The bin camera splits every weigh-in by ingredient. Food the vendor throws away at closing shows what to cook less of. Food customers leave on their plates shows which servings are too big. Bin weights are cooked weights; raw weights use each ingredient's cooked-to-raw ratio.
