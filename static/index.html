Leftover Scale

Connecting…

A food-waste bin with a weighing scale and a camera. It logs what gets thrown away so food-court vendors can see real daily demand and cook less surplus.

Weigh food into the bin

Each weigh-in is saved to the database. The bin camera takes a photo of each item. OpenAI checks whether it is food waste and which dish it comes from. Empty plates, bones and broth are not counted.

From bin to compost

Once food is weighed and logged, staff empty the bin into the compost bin instead of the general trash.
